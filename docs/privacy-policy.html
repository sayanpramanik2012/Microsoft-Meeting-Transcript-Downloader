Transcript Capture for Teams / Privacy

Privacy policy

Privacy, without the legal maze.

This extension has one job: help you save a Teams transcript. It works inside the tab you choose, and your transcript is not sent to us.

Local processingNo accountNo trackingNo ads
You start it

Nothing runs until you open the extension and select Start recorder.

It stays local

Transcript rows are assembled in the active browser tab, not on a server.

You own the file

The finished text file is saved by your browser and remains under your control.

What the extension reads

When you start the recorder on an open Microsoft Teams transcript, it reads only the information needed to build that transcript:

  • the words shown in the transcript;
  • speaker or display names;
  • timestamps and transcription start/stop messages; and
  • row numbers used to check whether any part of the transcript was missed.

A meeting can naturally contain personal, confidential, or sensitive information. The extension treats it all the same way: locally, and only for the download you requested.

What happens to a transcript

1. You select StartThe extension gets temporary access to the current tab.
→
2. Rows are assembledText is held briefly in that tab's memory and ordered for completeness.
→
3. You downloadMicrosoft Edge creates a plain-text file on your device.

Temporary captured rows are cleared when you stop the recorder, refresh or leave the page, or close the tab. A downloaded file stays on your device until you move or delete it.

What we never do

We do not receive, sell, share, or transfer your transcript. The extension has no developer server, analytics, advertising, tracking, external service, or remotely hosted executable code. Transcript information is not used for profiling, creditworthiness, or any purpose unrelated to creating your download.

Why Edge shows two permissions

activeTab

Gives the extension temporary access to the tab you selected after you invoke it. It does not provide ongoing access to every website.

scripting

Lets the extension place its packaged recorder inside that selected tab so it can respond to Start, Auto-capture, Download, and Stop.

The extension does not request browsing history, authentication data, persistent website access, background tab access, or extension storage.

You stay in control

You decide when the recorder starts, when auto-capture runs, whether to download an incomplete transcript, and when to stop. You can clear the temporary capture by selecting Stop recorder, refreshing or closing the tab, or removing the extension.

Please download only transcripts you are allowed to access, and protect saved files according to your organization’s policies.

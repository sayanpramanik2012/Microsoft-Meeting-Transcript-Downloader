Privacy policy

Transcript Capture for Teams processes your transcript inside the browser tab you choose. Nothing is sent to us.

Local onlyTranscript rows are assembled in the active tab.
No trackingNo analytics, advertising, accounts, or profiling.
User controlledYou start, download, stop, and delete the file.

Information the extension accesses

After you select Start recorder, the extension reads the open Teams transcript: its text, speaker or display names, timestamps, start/stop messages, and technical row numbers used to check coverage.

How the information is used

The information is held temporarily in the selected tab, placed in transcript order, and used to create the text file you request. Temporary capture data is cleared when you stop the recorder, refresh or leave the page, or close the tab. A downloaded file remains on your device until you move or delete it.

What is not collected or shared

The developer does not receive, store, sell, share, or transfer transcript information. The extension has no developer server, analytics, advertising, tracking, external service, or remotely hosted executable code. It does not request browsing history or authentication information.

Permissions and your choices

  • activeTab gives temporary access to the tab you selected after you invoke the extension.
  • scripting places the packaged recorder in that tab so the controls can work.

You control when capture starts, whether auto-capture runs, whether an incomplete transcript is downloaded, and when the recorder stops. Only capture transcripts you are authorized to access.

Questions and updates

Use the publisher support contact shown on the Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing for privacy or support questions. If these practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated before the change is released.
